# Runtime experiments

Experiments answer a named product question with a bounded, isolated implementation.
They do not create a second supported architecture by accumulating evidence files.

## Register before execution

Record the hypothesis, baseline, settings, source revision, acceptance criteria,
provider-request budget, owner and intended disposition in the relevant milestone.
Use deterministic fixtures and saved evidence before authorizing new inference.
Provider authentication remains with its supported owner. A research link does not
approve dependency installation, provider spending or a new product restriction.

## Isolation

Keep experimental source outside shipping imports. Reusable cases, graders and
sanitized outputs belong with their evaluation suite; private native logs, credentials
and operational receipts do not enter the repository. Keep negative and interrupted
outcomes in the accounting. Do not silently restart a stopped registration.

## Finish

Promote useful code into a supported owner with relevant tests, explicitly transfer
bounded work, or remove experimental executables/configuration and dependencies.
Retain a concise result and limitation in the owning milestone. Update the current
topic chapter for any adopted architecture. Git history holds superseded plans;
do not create dated reports, decision documents or evidence folders under `docs/`.

An inconclusive comparison does not block an independently useful feature without
acceptance-critical evidence. Follow the root [evidence and approval rules](../../AGENTS.md).
